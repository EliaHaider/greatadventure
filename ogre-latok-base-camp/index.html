Ogre & Latok Base Camp — 12 Days

A wild, little-visited trek up the Panmah Glacier from Askole to the base of the Ogre (Baintha Brakk, 7,285 m) and the Latok group — some of the most fiercely technical rock spires in the world, in a valley far quieter than the main Baltoro.

12 Days

This route is rated Challenging. Recommended for participants aged 18 and above who are in very good physical condition and ideally have prior glacier trekking experience — this is a genuinely remote route with far less foot traffic (and rescue infrastructure) than the main Baltoro treks. Expect long days over rough moraine and glacier ice, cold camping above 3,900 m for the best part of a week, and route-finding that depends on your guide's local knowledge. No technical climbing gear is required to reach base camp itself.
