Trek Packing List

Tick items off as you pack — your progress stays on this page while you're here.

This is the master list we give every Great Adventure trekker. Not every item applies to every trip — a short cultural trip to Shigar Valley needs a fraction of what a 3-week Baltoro expedition does, and the Gondogoro La items only apply if your itinerary crosses that pass. When in doubt, ask us before you buy.

Baggage & luggage limits

BagWho carries itWeight limit
Main baggage (duffel bag)Porter14 kg
DaypackYou (self-carry)5–7 kg
  • Main baggage — 14 kg limit, porter-carried. This is the duffel bag your porter carries for the whole trek, containing your sleeping bag, spare clothing, and everything you don't need during the walking day.
  • Daypack — 5–7 kg, self-carried. Carry this yourself each day: water, snacks, camera, rain jacket, sunscreen, and anything else you want quick access to on the trail.
  • Over the 14 kg main baggage limit? An extra charge applies per additional kg — porters are hired and paid by weight, so this isn't optional. Weigh your duffel before you leave home to avoid surprises in Skardu.
  • Don't need your city luggage on the trek? Leave it with us — we store it free of charge and securely at your Skardu hotel until you're back, so you're not paying a porter to carry clothes you won't wear on the trail.
