About us

Guided by someone who has actually walked the route.

Great Adventure was built on twenty-six years of field experience in Baltistan — not borrowed credentials, not a rebrand, but the direct, on-ground work of planning and leading trips through some of the world's highest terrain.

Before you travel

What foreign travellers ask us first.

The questions every international tourist has before booking a Pakistan trip.

Visa Assistance

We help with invitation letters and guidance for Pakistan's e-visa process for trekking and tourist visas. Exact process to confirm

Best Season

June to September is peak trekking season for K2 Base Camp, Concordia and the Gasherbrums. Deosai is best from July to early September.

Trip Insurance

High-altitude travel insurance with helicopter evacuation cover is strongly recommended for all treks above 4,000m. Confirm partner/provider if any

Transparent pricing

What's in the price, and what isn't.

General inclusions across most treks — each package's full itinerary page has the exact breakdown.

What's Included

  • 4x4 Jeep transport (Skardu to trailhead, return)
  • Professional trekking guide
  • Porters for group/camping equipment
  • 3-time meals during trek
  • Camping equipment (tents, kitchen, dining tent)
  • Hotel stay in Skardu (pre/post trek)
  • National Park / trekking permit fees

What's Not Included

  • Domestic flights (Islamabad – Skardu)
  • Personal porter (for personal duffel bag)
  • Climbing/technical gear rental
  • Travel & medical/evacuation insurance
  • Visa fees
  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Meals in Skardu outside the trek dates
