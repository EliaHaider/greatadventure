K2 Base Camp & Concordia

The classic Karakoram trek to the foot of the world's second-highest peak, through the Baltoro glacier and Concordia's amphitheatre of four 8,000m giants.

This route is rated Challenging. It's recommended for participants aged 18 and above (16+ possible with a guardian and prior trekking experience, assessed case by case) who are in very good physical condition. Expect back-to-back trekking days of 6–8+ hours over rocky glacier moraine, camping above 4,000 m for well over a week, and cold, remote conditions throughout. Prior high-altitude trekking experience and 2–3 months of dedicated cardio and endurance-hiking preparation beforehand are strongly recommended. No technical climbing gear is required, but acclimatisation discipline matters as much as leg fitness.

Day by day

Day 1

Islamabad to Skardu

Fly or drive to Skardu, the gateway town to the Karakoram. Evening briefing and gear check.

2,228 m
Day 2

Skardu — preparation day

Finalise permits, meet your guide and crew, last-minute supply shopping in Skardu bazaar.

2,228 m
Day 3

Skardu to Askole

A dramatic 4x4 jeep drive along the Shigar and Braldu valleys to Askole, the last village before the wilderness.

3,000 m
Day 4

Askole to Jhola

Trek begins. Follow the Braldu river through Korophon to your first wild camp.

3,150 m
Day 5

Jhola to Paiju

Continue along the valley floor to Paiju, the last campsite with trees before the glacier.

3,450 m
Day 6

Paiju — acclimatization day

Rest day to adjust to altitude before stepping onto the Baltoro glacier.

3,450 m
Day 7

Paiju to Khoburtse

First steps onto the Baltoro glacier itself, with early views of Trango Towers and Cathedral peaks.

3,700 m
Day 8

Khoburtse to Urdukas

Trek across moraine to Urdukas, known for its panoramic views back down the glacier.

3,900 m
Day 9

Urdukas — second acclimatization day

An extra rest day here, on the last patch of solid ground before the ice, further reduces altitude-related risk for the days ahead.

3,900 m
Day 10

Urdukas to Goro II

Continue on the glacier with Gasherbrum IV coming into view ahead.

4,300 m
Day 11

Goro II to Concordia

Arrive at Concordia, the confluence point surrounded by four of the world's fourteen 8,000m peaks.

4,600 m
Day 12

Concordia to K2 Base Camp and back

Day hike to K2 Base Camp for sunrise views of the mountain, returning to Concordia to camp.

5,150 m
Day 13

Concordia — reserve day

Built-in weather buffer day at the heart of the amphitheatre.

4,600 m
Day 14

Optional Broad Peak Base Camp side trip

A dedicated day to walk to Broad Peak Base Camp and back, rather than rushing it as an add-on.

4,950 m
Day 15

Concordia to Urdukas

Begin the return journey, retracing the route down the glacier.

3,900 m
Day 16

Urdukas to Jhola

Continue the descent back through Paiju towards Askole.

3,150 m
Day 17

Jhola to Askole, jeep to Skardu

Final trekking day back to Askole, then jeep transfer back to Skardu for a well-earned hot shower.

2,228 m
Day 18

Skardu — rest & recovery day

A full day to rest, wash gear and enjoy Skardu properly before flying out.

2,228 m
Day 19

Skardu — flight buffer day

Skardu–Islamabad flights are weather-dependent; this contingency day is built in to protect your onward international connections.

2,228 m
Day 20

Skardu to Islamabad

Fly or drive back to Islamabad. Trip ends.
