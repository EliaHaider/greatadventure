Nanga Parbat Base Camp — 9 Days

Trek to the foot of the world's ninth-highest peak — the legendary “Killer Mountain” — through the alpine meadows and pine forests of Fairy Meadows, on the Rakhiot side of Nanga Parbat (8,126 m).

This route is rated Moderate. It's the most accessible way to stand at the foot of an 8,000m peak — suitable for travellers aged 14 and above with a reasonable level of everyday fitness (comfortable with 4–6 hours of walking). No prior high-altitude or technical experience is required; road access via the Karakoram Highway also means far less remote logistics than our Baltoro treks.

Day by day

Day 1

Arrive Islamabad

Airport pickup, hotel check-in and evening trip briefing.

507 m
Day 2

Islamabad to Chilas

Long scenic drive on the Karakoram Highway via Besham and the Indus gorge.

1,250 m
Day 3

Chilas to Fairy Meadows

Drive to Raikot Bridge, jeep up to Tato village, then a forest hike into Fairy Meadows with your first close view of Nanga Parbat.

3,300 m
Day 4

Fairy Meadows to Nanga Parbat Base Camp

Trek via Beyal Camp along the Raikot Glacier to base camp, beneath the Rakhiot Face.

3,970 m
Day 5

Explore Base Camp

Rest and acclimatization day — optional short hikes toward the glacier for closer views of the "Killer Mountain".

3,970 m
Day 6

Base Camp back to Fairy Meadows

Retrace the route down to Fairy Meadows for a second night beneath the peak.

3,300 m
Day 7

Fairy Meadows to Chilas

Descend to Tato, jeep to Raikot Bridge, then drive on to Chilas.

1,250 m
Day 8

Chilas to Islamabad

Return drive on the Karakoram Highway.

507 m
Day 9

Departure

Free morning in Islamabad, transfer to the airport. Trip ends.
