Masherbrum (K1) Base Camp — 10 Days

Trek through the remote Hushe Valley of eastern Baltistan to the foot of Masherbrum (7,821 m) — the peak that first earned surveyors' “K” designation, long before K2 took the name.

10 Days

This route is rated Moderate. Locally considered one of the more approachable big-mountain base camp treks — suitable for travellers aged 14 and above with a reasonable level of fitness (comfortable with 4–6 hours of walking). Only a short, straightforward glacier crossing is involved before base camp; no technical climbing or rope skills are required.

Day by day

Day 1

Arrive Islamabad

Airport pickup, hotel check-in and evening trip briefing.

507 m
Day 2

Islamabad to Skardu

Fly or drive to Skardu, the gateway to Baltistan.

2,228 m
Day 3

Skardu to Khaplu

Drive along the Shyok river past the confluence with the Indus, visiting Khaplu Fort along the way.

2,560 m
Day 4

Khaplu to Hushe village

Continue by jeep up the Hushe valley to Hushe, the last village and gateway to Masherbrum.

3,050 m
Day 5

Hushe to Parbisan

Trek begins, following the Hushe river through pastures with Masherbrum ahead.

3,475 m
Day 6

Parbisan to Brumbrama

Continue along remote terrain parallel to the Masherbrum Glacier.

4,050 m
Day 7

Brumbrama to Masherbrum Base Camp

A short glacier-side walk brings you to base camp, with Masherbrum (K1) rising directly overhead.

4,280 m
Day 8

Explore Base Camp

Free day to enjoy views of Masherbrum, Laila Peak, K6 and K7, and visit the base camp lake.

4,280 m
Day 9

Trek back to Hushe

Retrace the route down to Hushe village, then jeep to Khaplu/Skardu.

2,228 m
Day 10

Skardu to Islamabad

Fly or drive back to Islamabad. Trip ends.
