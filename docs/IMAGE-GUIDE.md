# Great Adventure — Image Naming Guide

Jab bhi dost/uncle photos bhejein, unko **is naam se hi save** karke bhejein. Isse main seedha sahi jagah pe laga dunga, bina confusion ke.

Agar exact naam se save karna mushkil ho, to kam az kam **bata dein konsi photo kis category ki hai** (jese "ye 3 photos tours ke liye hain, ye 2 gallery ke liye").

---

## 1. Tour Card Photos (7 chahiye — 1 har tour ke liye)

| Filename | Kis Tour Ke Liye | Size/Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `tour-k2-base-camp.jpg` | K2 Base Camp & Concordia | Landscape (4:3), jese Concordia ya K2 ka view |
| `tour-deosai-plains.jpg` | Deosai Plains | Landscape, plains/wildlife/lake |
| `tour-gasherbrum-base-camp.jpg` | Gasherbrum Base Camp | Landscape, Gasherbrum peaks |
| `tour-trango-towers.jpg` | Trango Towers Base Camp | Landscape, Trango Towers rock face |
| `tour-shigar-valley.jpg` | Skardu & Shigar Valley | Landscape, fort/orchard/valley |
| `tour-gondogoro-la.jpg` | Gondogoro La Crossing | Landscape, pass/mountain view |
| `tour-custom.jpg` | Custom Itineraries | Koi bhi achi general trip photo |

---

## Quick Tips
- **Har photo ka size 500KB se kam** rakhein agar ho sake (mobile pe fast loading ke liye) — main resize/compress bhi kar sakta hoon agar aap raw photos bhej dein
- Landscape (horizontal) photos tour cards aur gallery ke liye behtar hain
- Agar kisi category ki photo abhi na ho, koi baat nahi — jo mil jaye pehle wahi bhej dein, baaki baad mein add kar denge
- Sab se **zaroori priority**: Tour card photos (7) aur Gallery (5) — inhe pehle bhejwane ki koshish karein

Jab bhi photos mile, seedha mujhe bhej dein (naam ke sath ya bina naam ke, bata dein kya hai) — main website mein daal dunga.
