K2 Base Camp & Concordia

The classic Karakoram trek to the foot of the world's second-highest peak, through the Baltoro glacier and Concordia's amphitheatre of four 8,000m giants.

16 Days

Day by day

Day 1

Islamabad to Skardu

Fly or drive to Skardu, the gateway town to the Karakoram. Evening briefing and gear check.

2,228 m
Day 2

Skardu — preparation day

Finalise permits, meet your guide and crew, last-minute supply shopping in Skardu bazaar.

2,228 m
Day 3

Skardu to Askole

A dramatic 4x4 jeep drive along the Shigar and Braldu valleys to Askole, the last village before the wilderness.

3,000 m
Day 4

Askole to Jhola

Trek begins. Follow the Braldu river through Korophon to your first wild camp.

3,150 m
Day 5

Jhola to Paiju

Continue along the valley floor to Paiju, the last campsite with trees before the glacier.

3,450 m
Day 6

Paiju — acclimatization day

Rest day to adjust to altitude before stepping onto the Baltoro glacier.

3,450 m
Day 7

Paiju to Khoburtse

First steps onto the Baltoro glacier itself, with early views of Trango Towers and Cathedral peaks.

3,700 m
Day 8

Khoburtse to Urdukas

Trek across moraine to Urdukas, known for its panoramic views back down the glacier.

3,900 m
Day 9

Urdukas to Goro II

Continue on the glacier with Gasherbrum IV coming into view ahead.

4,300 m
Day 10

Goro II to Concordia

Arrive at Concordia, the confluence point surrounded by four of the world's fourteen 8,000m peaks.

4,600 m
Day 11

Concordia to K2 Base Camp and back

Day hike to K2 Base Camp for sunrise views of the mountain, returning to Concordia to camp.

5,150 m
Day 12

Concordia — reserve day

Weather buffer day, or optional side trip to Broad Peak Base Camp.

4,600 m
Day 13

Concordia to Urdukas

Begin the return journey, retracing the route down the glacier.

3,900 m
Day 14

Urdukas to Jhola

Continue the descent back through Paiju towards Askole.

3,150 m
Day 15

Jhola to Askole, jeep to Skardu

Final trekking day back to Askole, then jeep transfer back to Skardu for a well-earned hot shower.

2,228 m
Day 16

Skardu to Islamabad

Fly or drive back to Islamabad. Trip ends.
