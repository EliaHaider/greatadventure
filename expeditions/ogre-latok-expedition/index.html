About this expedition

The Ogre (Baintha Brakk, 7,285 m) and the Latok group are a cluster of dramatic rock peaks in the Panmah Muztagh in the central Karakoram. The Latok peaks lie just east of the Ogre; the Baintha Lukpar Glacier, a small tributary of the Biafo, lies to their south and the Choktoi Glacier to the north. All of the summits are notable for their extreme technical difficulty.

The approach is short for a Karakoram objective: three days from Askole via Namla and Mango on the Biafo Glacier to a green base camp at about 4,200 to 4,500 metres. Our standard programme is 27 days, with 16 days at base camp for climbing, and covers the flights, permits, porters, base camp and all logistics from Skardu. The length and the objective can be adapted to your team.

Who this expedition is for

Experienced rock, ice and alpine climbers who want a serious technical objective in a quiet corner of the Karakoram. Climbing is not guided: your team leads the climbing, and our staff run the camp, kitchen and support behind you.

Route notes

  • Askole, Namla, Mango and base camp over three days on and beside the Biafo Glacier.
  • Base camp at about 4,200 to 4,500 metres, with fresh water and grassy ground.
  • Sixteen days on the mountain in the standard 27-day programme.
  • Peaks: Ogre 7,285 m, Latok I 7,145 m, Latok II 7,108 m, Latok III 6,949 m, Latok IV 6,456 m and many smaller objectives.

Peaks and history

1977 — Ogre first climbed. Doug Scott and Chris Bonington made the first ascent of the Ogre, followed by a descent that became one of mountaineering's famous survival stories after Scott broke both legs.

1977 — Latok II. An Italian group led by Arturo Bergamaschi made the first ascent of Latok II (7,108 m) by the southeast face.

1979 — Latok I and Latok III. Japanese expeditions made the first ascents of Latok I (7,145 m) and Latok III. Latok IV (6,456 m) followed in 1980.

Common questions

Is this a trek or an expedition?

It is a climbing expedition. The three-day walk to base camp is part of it, and the climbing period at base camp is where the objective is. If you only want to visit base camp, contact us and we will quote that separately.

Is climbing guided?

No. Our service is logistics, permits and base camp support. Your team does the climbing.

How long is the expedition?

The standard plan is 27 days from Islamabad, with 16 days at base camp. We can adapt it to your team and objective.

When is the best time to go?

Summer. The climbing window is usually June to August.

What permits do I need?

The climbing permit and Central Karakoram National Park fees are arranged for you, together with the official briefing and debriefing.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The fair price depends on group size, dates, porter allowance, oxygen choice and the level of high-altitude support. We prefer to understand your team first and then send a clear written proposal.
