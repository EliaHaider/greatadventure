About this expedition

Broad Peak is an 8,000-metre peak beside K2, reached by the classic Baltoro approach through Askole, Paiju, Urdukas, Goro II and Concordia. The normal route is the West Ridge, climbed from a base camp on the Godwin-Austen Glacier.

It is generally thought of as less technical than K2, but the route still means crevasse management, fixed lines, high camps and a long, exposed summit day. This is a mountain for experienced climbers stepping into 8,000 m terrain, not a first expedition.

Who this expedition is for

Climbers with previous high-altitude expedition experience who want a major Karakoram objective with full local logistics. You should be confident with crampons, fixed ropes and glacier travel, and able to look after yourself in cold, remote conditions.

Route notes

  • Approach along the Baltoro Glacier to a base camp near K2 Base Camp.
  • Normal plan uses staged camps on the standard West Ridge route.
  • Weeks of acclimatisation rotations, rest days and weather holds before any summit attempt.

Mountain history

9 June 1957 — First ascent. Hermann Buhl, Kurt Diemberger, Marcus Schmuck and Fritz Wintersteller made the first ascent. The small Austrian team climbed without supplementary oxygen and without high-altitude porters.

5 March 2013 — First winter ascent. Four Polish climbers reached the main summit in winter. Two of them, Maciej Berbeka and Tomasz Kowalski, died on the descent.

Common questions

Is Broad Peak easier than K2?

It is usually considered less technical than K2, but it is still an 8,000 m expedition with extreme altitude, glacier travel, high camps and weather exposure.

Which route is used?

The normal West Ridge route, from base camp on the Godwin-Austen Glacier.

What experience do I need?

Previous high-altitude expedition experience, solid fixed-line skills and the ability to live and climb independently in severe conditions.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The fair price depends on group size, dates, porter allowance, oxygen choice and the level of high-altitude support. We prefer to understand your team first and then send a clear written proposal.

Are permits included?

Yes. We handle the mountaineering permit, peak royalty, environment fees, liaison officer coordination and the official briefing and debriefing. We need your passport copies, visa details and insurance documents well before arrival.

  • Personal satellite phone, internet and extra battery charging
  • Extra hotel nights, meals and transport caused by weather, flight cancellations or road closures
  • Rescue, helicopter evacuation, hospital and repatriation costs
  • Tips and summit bonuses for guides, porters and kitchen staff
  • Anything not listed under What's Included or in your written proposal
