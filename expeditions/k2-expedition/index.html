About this expedition

K2 rises to 8,611 metres in the Karakoram. This expedition is for experienced mountaineers who need dependable Pakistan-based logistics for the Baltoro approach, K2 Base Camp and the normal Abruzzi Spur on the Pakistan side of the mountain.

The programme starts in Islamabad, moves through Skardu and Askole and follows the classic Baltoro route through Paiju, Urdukas, Goro II and Concordia. From K2 Base Camp the climbing plan is shaped by weather, route condition, team strength and the decisions of the expedition leader.

Who this expedition is for

Experienced high-altitude climbers with expedition living, glacier travel, fixed-line movement and good self-care at altitude behind them, and the discipline to make conservative decisions under pressure. Turning around is a normal part of climbing K2.

Route notes

  • Local Askole and Skardu operations for porters, jeeps, food, base camp staff and government procedures.
  • Several acclimatisation rotations before any summit attempt.
  • Summit strategy follows conditions on the Abruzzi Spur, not fixed calendar promises. The itinerary is a planning framework.

Mountain history

31 July 1954 — First ascent. Achille Compagnoni and Lino Lacedelli reached the summit by the Abruzzi Spur, as part of the Italian expedition led by Ardito Desio.

16 January 2021 — First winter ascent. Ten Nepali climbers summited together, completing the first winter ascent of K2, the last 8,000-metre peak to be climbed in winter.

Common questions

What is the best season for K2?

Summer, when the Baltoro approach is open and teams have the best chance of usable weather windows at altitude.

Which route is used?

The Abruzzi Spur on the Pakistan side, approached through Skardu, Askole, the Baltoro Glacier, Concordia and K2 Base Camp.

Do I need previous 8,000 m experience?

It is strongly recommended. At minimum you need serious high-altitude expedition experience, strong fixed-line skills and excellent self-care at altitude.

Is the summit guaranteed?

No. K2 depends on weather, avalanche and rockfall risk, team health and the leader's judgement. A safe descent always comes first.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The fair price depends on group size, dates, porter allowance, oxygen choice and the level of high-altitude support. We prefer to understand your team first and then send a clear written proposal.

Are permits included?

Yes. We handle the mountaineering permit, peak royalty, environment fees, liaison officer coordination and the official briefing and debriefing. We need your passport copies, visa details and insurance documents well before arrival.

  • Rescue, helicopter evacuation, hospital and repatriation costs
  • Tips and summit bonuses for guides, porters and kitchen staff
