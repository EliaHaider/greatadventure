About the Gondogoro La crossing trek

This 21-day trek is the full Karakoram journey: Islamabad, Skardu, Askole, the Baltoro Glacier, Concordia, K2 Base Camp, Ali Camp, the Gondogoro La pass, Khuspang, Saicho, Hushe and back to Skardu. It is a hard, high-altitude trek on the classic route.

Great Adventure runs this trek from Skardu with the guide, cook, camp crew, drivers and porter team organised directly for your group. From Concordia we continue to K2 Base Camp and the Art Gilkey Memorial, then return toward Concordia and move to Ali Camp for the alpine start over Gondogoro La. In suitable weather the pass gives one of the finest dawn panoramas in the Karakoram before the descent into the Hushe valley.

Who this trek is for

Strong hikers and experienced trekkers who want the full Baltoro, K2 Base Camp and Gondogoro La experience. You must be comfortable walking for long days on rocky moraine, glacier, loose trail and snow. It is not a casual sightseeing tour: the route reaches 5,585 m, includes remote camping and an early-morning pass crossing where patience and teamwork matter as much as speed.

Route notes

  • A complete Baltoro journey from Askole to K2 Base Camp, followed by the Gondogoro La crossing when conditions and team strength allow.
  • Jeep road, moraine trails and long glacier days on the Baltoro. The Gondogoro La section is steep, exposed and condition-dependent.
  • If the pass is unsafe, the guide team may switch to an out-and-back K2 Base Camp plan.
  • Hotels in Islamabad and Skardu. Tented camps with a mess tent, cooked meals and boiled water on the trek.

About the Gondogoro La pass

Where it is — A pass between two glaciers. Gondogoro La (5,585 m) lies about 25 km south of K2. It joins the Gondogoro Glacier on the Hushe side with the Vigne Glacier on the Concordia side. In Balti, la means a mountain pass.

The crossing — Sunrise from Ali Camp. The crossing starts in the early hours from Ali Camp so the snow is firm and the rocks above the descent are still frozen. At the top the view takes in K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I to IV, Masherbrum and Laila Peak.

The descent — Harder than the climb. The way up is a moderate snow slope. The descent to Khuspang is steep, with loose rock and scree, so concentration and good boots matter. Rockfall is the main objective danger later in the day.

Best time — Late June to August. Early in the season the snow cover is firmer. By August melting raises the risk of crevasses, rockfall and avalanche, so route conditions are checked before committing to the pass.

Experience — Fitness, acclimatisation and judgement. You do not need to be a technical climber, but you need top fitness, prior acclimatisation and the right equipment. A harness, crampons and an ascender may be needed for the roped sections.

Common questions

How hard is the Gondogoro La crossing?

It is the hardest day of the trek. You start around 1–2 AM, climb a snow slope to 5,585 m and then make a steep, demanding descent to Khuspang. Good fitness and acclimatisation matter more than climbing skill.

When is the best time to cross Gondogoro La?

Late June to August. Early season snow is firmer; by August melting raises the risk of crevasses and rockfall.

Do I need crampons and a harness?

They may be required depending on conditions. We confirm the final technical gear list before departure.

What if the pass is unsafe?

The crossing is only made when conditions allow. If weather, snow, rockfall risk or group health makes it unsafe, the guide team may switch to an out-and-back K2 Base Camp plan.

How many days does the trek take?

The full itinerary is 21 days from Islamabad, including acclimatisation at Paiju, a rest day in Skardu and a buffer day.

Where does the trek start and end?

It starts in Askole, follows the Baltoro Glacier to Concordia and K2 Base Camp, crosses Gondogoro La and finishes in Hushe, then a drive to Skardu.

Is this the same as the K2 Base Camp trek?

It includes the K2 Base Camp route and adds the Gondogoro La pass crossing. Our K2 Base Camp & Concordia trek is the shorter version without the pass.

Are flights, permits and insurance included?

The domestic flights between Islamabad and Skardu, trekking permits and park fees are included. Your travel insurance is mandatory and must cover high-altitude trekking and helicopter evacuation, valid to at least 6,000 m.

How should I prepare?

Train for 10 to 12 weeks with steady uphill walking, long descents and back-to-back days. Bring broken-in boots, a warm sleeping bag, layers, a waterproof shell, gloves, a headlamp and poles.
