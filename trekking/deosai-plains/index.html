About this trek

Deosai Plains, known as the Land of Giants, offers an immersive adventure across vast grasslands, high-altitude lakes and diverse wildlife. This 5-day camping trek is ideal for nature lovers, trekkers and photographers.

The trek starts and ends in Skardu. You drive into Deosai National Park, walk from Chota Pani to Bara Pani and on to Sheosar Lake at 4,200 m, with Nanga Parbat on the horizon, and return through Kala Pani. Deosai is best from June to September, when the plateau is open and in bloom.

Who this trek is for

Trekkers with basic fitness, photographers and families who want a gentle introduction to high-altitude camping in Baltistan, without long glacier days.

Route notes

  • Altitude range from about 2,200 m in Skardu to 4,200 m at Sheosar Lake.
  • Camping trek: nights at Bara Pani (3,800 m) and Sheosar Lake (4,200 m).
  • Activities: trekking, wildlife spotting, photography and camping.
  • Best season: June to September.

Highlights

Bara Pani — The largest river in Deosai. A broad river crossing the plateau at 3,800 m, with a camp and a spectacular sunset.

Sheosar Lake — The jewel of Deosai. An alpine lake at 4,200 m with views toward Nanga Parbat and a good chance of spotting wildlife.

Wildlife — Land of Giants. Deosai National Park is known for its high-altitude wildlife and wide open grassland.

Day by day

Day 1

Arrive in Skardu

Arrive in Skardu (by flight from Islamabad), settle into the hotel, then visit Skardu bazaar and Buddha Rock and enjoy a traditional Balti dinner.

2,228 m
Day 2

Drive to Deosai and trek to Bara Pani

A drive of about 2.5 hours from Skardu into Deosai National Park, then a trek from Chota Pani across wide meadows and glacial streams to Bara Pani, the largest river in Deosai. Camp for the night with a sunset over the plateau.

3,800 m
Day 3

Trek to Sheosar Lake

Walk across the high plains with views of Nanga Parbat to Sheosar Lake, the jewel of Deosai. Time to relax, take photographs and watch for wildlife before camping by the lake.

4,200 m
Day 4

Sunrise at the lake, return to Skardu

Watch the sunrise over Sheosar Lake, then trek back through Kala Pani to the exit point and drive to Skardu with scenic stops. A farewell Balti dinner in the evening.

2,228 m
Day 5

Departure from Skardu

Transfer to Skardu airport (flight to Islamabad) for your onward journey.

2,228 m

Common questions

How long is the Deosai trek?

The trek is 5 days and 4 nights, starting and ending in Skardu.

What is the best time to go?

June to September, when the plateau is open and the meadows are in bloom.

How hard is it?

Moderate. The walking days are manageable, but you camp at 3,800 m to 4,200 m, so nights are cold.

Is the Islamabad flight included?

Yes. The domestic flight between Islamabad and Skardu is included, weather permitting.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The fair price depends on group size, dates, porter allowance, oxygen choice and the level of high-altitude support. We prefer to understand your team first and then send a clear written proposal.
