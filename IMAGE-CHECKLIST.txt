GREAT ADVENTURE — IMAGE CHECKLIST
====================================
Every image the website code currently expects, exactly where to save
it, and what it should show. Save each file inside an "images" folder
at the very top level of your site (same level as index.html).

lowercase, same .jpg extension) or the image will not load.

1. images/logo/logo.png
-------------------------
Your Great Adventure logo. Used in: the header on every page, the
footer on every page, the browser tab icon (favicon), and the admin
panel. PNG with a transparent background works best (it sits on both
light and dark backgrounds across the site).

3. images/gallery/  — the "Gallery" section on the homepage — NOW FLEXIBLE

This changed from fixed names to simple numbers, so you can add as
many (or as few) photos as you like without needing any code changes:

  - Save your photos as 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg, 4.jpg, 5.jpg, 6.jpg... in
    order — any number, in any amount, up to 20.jpg.
  - You do NOT need all 20 — the site automatically hides any number
    that doesn't exist. Even just 1.jpg alone works fine.
  - 1.jpg is always shown bigger than the rest — pick your single best
    photo for that slot.
  - The first 5 that exist (1–5) show right away. Anything from 6
    onward sits behind a "View more photos" button, which only
    appears if you've actually added a 6th photo or beyond.
  - Square-ish or landscape photos work better than tall portrait
    shots, since they display in a fixed-height grid.

Good things to photograph for this section: the Baltoro glacier, K2 at
sunrise, a campsite (e.g. Urdukas), the Deosai plateau, Shigar valley,
Concordia's four-8000m-peak view, Trango Towers, the Gondogoro La
crossing, Askole village life, and the night sky over Deosai.
