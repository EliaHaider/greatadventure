Gasherbrum I & II Expedition

Trek to the foot of Gasherbrum I and II — two of the region's 8,000m giants — via the Baltoro and Abruzzi glaciers.

Summit Altitude
This is rated Extreme — a genuine 8,000m mountaineering expedition, not a trek. Participants must have prior high-altitude climbing experience (ideally a previous 7,000m+ summit), be competent on fixed ropes, crampons and jumar/ascender technique, and be in exceptional physical condition. Expect 3–4 weeks at Base Camp with repeated acclimatization rotations, technical sections above 7,000 m, and extended exposure to extreme cold and altitude. We strongly recommend climbing with a certified high-altitude guide (IFMGA or equivalent) for anyone without recent 8,000m experience — ask us about arranging one.

Day 12–37

Gasherbrum I & II climbing period

Our climbing Sirdar leads acclimatization rotations through the lower and upper camps on each peak, fixing rope on technical sections and tracking forecasts for a summit window on both Gasherbrum I (8,080m) and Gasherbrum II (8,035m). This block is intentionally not broken into individual days — summit timing depends entirely on each season's weather, and built-in contingency days absorb delays on both peaks.
