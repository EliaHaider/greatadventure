Gasherbrum Base Camp — 18 Days

Trek to the foot of Gasherbrum I and II — two of the region's 8,000m giants — via the Baltoro and Abruzzi glaciers.

18 Days

This route is rated Challenging. It's recommended for participants aged 18 and above (16+ possible with a guardian and prior trekking experience, assessed case by case) who are in very good physical condition. Expect back-to-back trekking days of 6–8+ hours over rocky glacier moraine, camping above 4,000 m for well over a week, and cold, remote conditions throughout. Prior high-altitude trekking experience and 2–3 months of dedicated cardio and endurance-hiking preparation beforehand are strongly recommended. No technical climbing gear is required, but acclimatisation discipline matters as much as leg fitness.
