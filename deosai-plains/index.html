Deosai Plains — 4 Days

A high-altitude alpine plateau known for brown bears, wildflowers and some of the clearest night skies in the region — a gentler alternative to glacier treks.

4 Days

Max Altitude
This route is rated Easy. It suits most reasonably active travellers, including families with children (roughly 8 years and up) and first-time visitors to the region. Days are short drives with light walking rather than sustained high-altitude trekking, and overnight altitude stays well under 4,150 m. No prior trekking experience is required — just the fitness to comfortably walk for an hour or two.
