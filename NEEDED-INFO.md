# Great Adventure — Missing Information Checklist

Ye file un sab cheezon ki list hai jo abhi website mein **placeholder** ke roop mein hain. Jab dost se ye info mil jaye, mujhe bata dein — main seedha website mein daal dunga.

Website mein jahan bhi ye info missing hai, wahan halke dashed-border boxes ya "needed" tags dikhte hain — taake dost ko pata chale kya kya bharna hai.

---

## 1. Legal / Trust Info
- [ ] GB Tourism Department **registration number**
- [ ] PATO (Pakistan Association of Tour Operators) membership — hai ya nahi, number
- [ ] BAATO (Adventure Tour Operators) membership — hai ya nahi, number
- [ ] Company ka poora legal/registered naam (agar "Great Adventure" se alag ho)
- [ ] Office address (Skardu mein exact location, agar physical office hai)

## 2. Contact Details
- [ ] WhatsApp number (jo website + floating button dono mein use hoga)
- [ ] Email address (confirm: hello@greatadventure.com ya koi aur)
- [ ] Facebook Page link
- [ ] Instagram handle
- [ ] Office ka Google Maps pin / exact coordinates

## 3. Team
- [ ] Uncle (founder) ka naam, , **photo**
- [ ] Baaki guides/staff ke naam, designation, **photos** (kam se kam lead guide + coordinator)
- [ ] Sab ka ek chhota intro (1-2 lindesignatione, jese "10 saal se Baltoro route guide kar rahe hain")

## 4. Tours — Har Tour Ke Liye
Har tour card ke liye ye chahiye (abhi sirf duration hai, baaki sab placeholder hai):
- [ ] **Price** (per person, PKR ya USD mein)
- [ ] Group size limit (min/max logon ki tadad)
- [ ] Exact inclusions (guide, porter, meals, camping gear — kya kya shamil hai)
- [ ] Exact exclusions (kya tourist ko khud arrange karna hoga)
- [ ] Ek achi **photo** har tour card ke liye

Konse 6 tours abhi list kiye hain (confirm/correct karein):
1. K2 Base Camp & Concordia — 16 days
2. Deosai Plains — 4 days
3. Nanga Parbat Base Camp — 7 days
4. Skardu & Shigar Valley — 3 days
5. Gondogoro La Crossing — 18 days
6. Custom itineraries — flexible

*(Agar ye list sahi nahi ya koi tour missing hai to batayein)*

## 5. Photos &amp; Videos (Sab Se Zaroori)
- [ ] Har tour ke liye kam az kam 1 achi photo (upar list mein 6 chahiye)
- [ ] Gallery ke liye 5-10 landscape/action photos (Baltoro, K2, Deosai, Urdukas camp, Shigar valley)
- [ ] Founder/team photos (professional ya casual, achi lighting)
- [ ] Agar koi drone/trip video hai, wo bhi (hero section mein background video daal sakte hain)

## 6. Testimonials
- [ ] Purane tourists ke 3+ asal reviews/messages
- [ ] Unka naam + country (permission ke sath)
- [ ] Agar unki photo mil sake to aur behtar (nahi to sirf naam/country chalega)

## 7. Visa &amp; Travel Info
- [ ] Visa assistance ka exact process (kya company invitation letter deti hai, ya sirf guidance)
- [ ] Koi specific documents jo tourist ko chahiye honge

## 8. News/Blog Content (Optional, Baad Mein)
- [ ] 2-3 chhoti updates likhne ke liye (trail conditions, season updates, koi naya route)
- [ ] In posts ke liye photos aur dates

## 9. Business Decisions
- [ ] Domain confirm: greatadventure.com available hai ya variation chahiye hogi
- [ ] Payment method — online advance (Wise/Payoneer) chahiye ya sirf on-arrival
- [ ] Cancellation/refund policy

---

**Priority order agar sab ek sath nahi mil sakta:**
1. WhatsApp number + Email
2. Tour photos + prices
3. Team photos + names
4. Registration numbers
5. Testimonials
6. Baaki sab (news, videos, visa detail)
